# UtilityCraft
UtilityCraft is a Minecraft Bedrock add-on, also categorized as an addonpack, that brings QoL changes, new utilities and a whole new progression involving machines and energy.

Its main features include:
- New Tools — Paxels, Hammers and AIOTs for conveninence and more uses.
- More Seeds — A huge expansion for the harvest system, with more seeds and soils.
- Bonsais — A compact block for simple and easy farming for plants.
- Elevators — Blocks that help you go up and down on your base.
- Machines, Generators and an Energy System — An entirely new system, focusing on mass production, complex setups and more objectives before reaching the end-game.
And much more.
